About this demo. ding-dong is a working name for a hyperlocal delivery app being piloted in Porur, Chennai 600116. This page runs the actual web app in a sandbox: the stores, products, riders and orders are sample data kept in your own browser, so you can place orders, accept them as a seller and deliver them as a rider without touching anyone's real account. Sign-in is automatic with demo numbers. Nothing you do here is sent to the live service, and real SMS codes are never used.

Voice ordering uses your browser's speech recognition (best in Chrome). Prices, ETAs and partners are illustrative.
